AUX1 and AUX2 work only from the board's own terminal; the dashboard never drives them. If you wire them anyway, mark the two leads with tape — jumper kits come in the same ten colours.

Before you trust a lead

  • Go by position, not colour. Dyes vary: on one real cable GND's brown passed for grey and +5V's orange read as pink.
  • Keep GND clear of both supplies. The dashboard switches +3.3V and +5V on together.
  • Measure when unsure. Clip the yellow ADC lead to a supply lead and read it in Pins & power → Measure a voltage: about 3.3 V or 5.0 V.
